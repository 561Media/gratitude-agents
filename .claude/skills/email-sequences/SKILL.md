# Email Sequences Skill

## Purpose
Build complete email sequences that move subscribers toward a specific outcome:
a first activation, a first or recurring funding commitment, partner onboarding,
or ongoing retention.

## Context
Brand memory, voice, positioning, messaging framework, and terminology are
already loaded above. Follow them exactly.

## Locked Language (Activate + Fund)
- Activate: a person chooses an available pre-funded act. It costs them nothing at the moment. Activate is the dominant action.
- Fund: creates capacity for future acts, once or recurring. "Fund" is an approved verb. Keep it clearly distinct from Activate.
- Never "donate" or "donation" for either action. Fund receipt and legal language is still to be confirmed: do not promise tax treatment.
- Sponsors appear only as optional attribution ("Made possible by [Sponsor]") or as one funding type. Never "Companies sponsor, people activate."
- Express is NEXT and Facilitate is FUTURE. Never present them as available now.
- Never invent numbers, results, deadlines, or partner names. Use supplied facts or [NEEDS INPUT].
- No em dashes.

## Sequence Types

**Activator Welcome Sequence (4 emails)**
Goal: get a new participant to complete their first activation.
- Email 1: Welcome. Good is ready to happen: choose an act, no payment required. One CTA.
- Email 2 (Day 2): How it works. Pre-funded, delivered by a trusted nonprofit partner, verified.
- Email 3 (Day 4): What happens after you activate. Show impact, not a receipt.
- Email 4 (Day 7): Invite to activate again.

**Funder Welcome Sequence (5 emails)**
Goal: turn a new funder lead into a first funding commitment.
- Email 1: Welcome. Funding creates capacity for future acts, once or recurring.
- Email 2 (Day 2): Why Gratitude.com exists. Most giving ends with a receipt; Gratitude ends with a result.
- Email 3 (Day 4): The mechanism in plain language: Pre-fund, Activate, Deliver, Verify, Show Impact, Refill.
- Email 4 (Day 6): Proof. Verified results only if supplied, otherwise [NEEDS INPUT].
- Email 5 (Day 8): Soft CTA. Fund an act or schedule a conversation.

**Funder Result Loop (ongoing, after funding)**
Goal: show funders what their funding made possible and invite a Refill.
- Trigger: capacity they funded is activated, delivered, and verified.
- Email: what was activated, who delivered it, how it was verified, with privacy and dignity protected.
- Milestone email: capacity used; invite them to refill (once or recurring).

**Partner Onboarding Sequence (5 emails)**
Goal: welcome a new trusted nonprofit partner and set up success.
- Email 1: Welcome. What to expect.
- Email 2 (Day 3): How pre-funded capacity reaches them and how activations arrive.
- Email 3 (Day 5): Delivery and verification that protect privacy and dignity.
- Email 4 (Day 7): What participants and funders see after delivery.
- Email 5 (Day 10): Partnership kickoff and next steps.

**Sponsoring Organization Sequence (7 emails)**
Goal: help an organization create funded capacity its people can activate.
- Email 1: Open the loop. Their people care; the missing piece is infrastructure.
- Email 2: The cost of programs that end with a receipt.
- Email 3: Sponsored capacity: they fund acts their employees, customers, or community can activate. No pricing yet.
- Email 4: Proof. Verified outcomes only if supplied, otherwise [NEEDS INPUT].
- Email 5: Objection handling. Address the top 3.
- Email 6: Timing. Use a real deadline only if one exists.
- Email 7: Last call. Final CTA with a clear close.

**Nurture Sequence (ongoing)**
Goal: stay top of mind, build trust, drive eventual action.
- Cadence: 1 to 2 emails per week
- Format: one idea, one insight, one action
- Rule of 3: 2 value emails for every 1 ask

## For Each Email, Deliver:
- Subject line (primary)
- Subject line (A/B variant)
- Preview text
- Full email body
- CTA (text and link placeholder)

## Voice Rules for Email
- No "Hope this finds you well" openers
- Open with a hook, a question, or a bold statement
- One idea per email
- One CTA per email
- Plain text preferred over heavy HTML unless specified
- Sign off from a real person, not a brand name
- Locked language: "activate" never "donate", "fund" for creating capacity
- No em dashes

## Output Format
Numbered sequence. One email per section. Estimated send day noted.
