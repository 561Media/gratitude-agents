# Direct Response Copy Skill

## Purpose
Write copy that moves people to act, not copy that only sounds polished.

## Context
Brand memory, voice, positioning, terminology, and messaging constraints are
already loaded above. Follow them exactly.

## Locked Language (Activate + Fund)
- Activate: a person chooses an available pre-funded act. It costs them nothing at the moment. Activate is the dominant action.
- Fund: creates capacity for future acts, once or recurring. "Fund" is an approved verb. Keep it clearly distinct from Activate.
- Never "donate" or "donation" for either action.
- Sponsors appear only as optional attribution ("Made possible by [Sponsor]") or as one funding type (organizations creating funded capacity for their employees, customers, or communities). Never "Companies sponsor, people activate."
- Express is NEXT and Facilitate is FUTURE. Never present them as available now.
- Never invent numbers, results, testimonials, or partner names. Use supplied facts or [NEEDS INPUT].
- No em dashes.

## Before Writing, Confirm:
1. What is the offer or message?
2. Who is the reader? Branch the copy by reader type:
   - **Participant (activator):** a person choosing an available pre-funded act. Goal: first activation.
   - **Funder:** an individual, company, or foundation creating capacity for future acts. Goal: fund, once or recurring.
   - **Sponsoring organization:** creates funded capacity for its employees, customers, or community. Goal: set up sponsored capacity.
   - **Trusted nonprofit partner:** delivers acts and supports verification. Goal: partnership conversation.
   Be specific within the type ("foundation program officer", "people team lead at a 2,000-person company").
3. What action should they take?
4. What awareness level are they at?
   - Unaware: does not know good intention can have somewhere to go
   - Problem-aware: feels that giving ends with a receipt and no result
   - Solution-aware: knows pre-funded or verified giving models exist, has not chosen one
   - Product-aware: knows Gratitude.com, has not activated or funded yet
   - Most aware: ready to act, needs a reason now

## Frameworks (select based on awareness level)

**Problem-aware or lower: PAS**
- Problem: name the gap clearly (intention with nowhere to go, a receipt instead of a result)
- Agitate: make it feel real without guilt or pressure
- Solution: present Gratitude.com's mechanism as the obvious answer

**Solution-aware: AIDA**
- Attention: interrupt the scroll
- Interest: build relevance
- Desire: make them want the result
- Action: one clear CTA

**Most aware: Before / After / Bridge**
- Before: where they are now
- After: where they want to be (a verified result they can see)
- Bridge: how Gratitude.com gets them there (Pre-fund, Activate, Deliver, Verify, Show Impact, Refill)

## Output for Landing Pages
1. Hero headline (primary hook, under 12 words)
2. Subheadline (one sentence)
3. 3 lead benefits (outcome-focused, not feature-focused)
4. Social proof block (supplied testimonial or stat, otherwise a clearly marked [NEEDS INPUT] slot)
5. CTA button copy (action verb + outcome). Activate is primary; Fund is secondary.
6. Objection-handling section (3 most common objections for this reader type + responses)
7. Secondary CTA

## Output for Ads
- 3 headline variations (per platform character limits)
- 2 body copy variations (short and long)
- 1 CTA per variation

## Quality Check
Before delivering, verify:
- No generic openers ("Are you tired of...?")
- No passive CTAs ("Learn more," "Click here")
- No unsubstantiated superlatives or invented proof
- Every line earns its place
- Voice matches brand memory
- Locked language is used: "activate" never "donate", "fund" for creating capacity, sponsors only as attribution or a funding type
- No em dashes
