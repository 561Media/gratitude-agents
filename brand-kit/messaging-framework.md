# Gratitude.com: Messaging Framework

Source: System Story v3 (LOCKED) and MVP UX Story. Quote locked lines as written. Do not invent taglines, stats, or proof points. Investor messaging lives in `investor-core.yaml`.

## Core Message Hierarchy

### Level 1: Category (the close)
THE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR HUMAN ACKNOWLEDGMENT.
Signature beneath it: **Gratitude, delivered.**

### Level 2: Value Proposition
A system that turns human intention into real-world action, and makes the impact visible. One infrastructure. Multiple ways in. Real-world impact out.

### Level 3: The MVP promise (consumer)
We deliver gratitude into the world. Real acts of good are already funded and ready. Choose one. Put it into motion.
Core product truth: It is funded. I choose. It moves. I see what happened.

## Sequencing (never blur this)

| Stage | Participation | Line |
|---|---|---|
| NOW (MVP) | **Activate** (dominant) | Trigger good. Choose an available pre-funded act of real-world impact. |
| NOW (MVP) | **Fund** (secondary) | Make more possible. Create capacity for future acts, once or recurring. |
| NEXT | Express | Acknowledge someone. Not in MVP copy or flows. |
| FUTURE | Facilitate | Help deliver good. |

Participate in one way or many. No single behavior is required. The Commitment (Notice. Acknowledge. Choose consciously. Take responsibility. Contribute where possible.) is the philosophical foundation, never an onboarding gate or pledge.

## Supporting Messages (by audience)

**For Participants (activators)**
Real acts of good are already funded and ready. Choose one. Put it into motion. No payment is required to activate an act. It is funded; your choice gives the go-ahead. Then follow the good until it lands.

**For Funders (individuals, companies, foundations)**
Fund the next act. Your contribution helps put real acts into the system before anyone clicks. Then someone else gets to choose one and make it happen. And the funder sees what their funding made possible. (Charitable receipt and legal language to be confirmed. Do not claim tax deductibility.)

Organizations can create funded capacity for their employees, customers, or communities to activate. Attribution stays subtle: "Made possible by [Sponsor]." Sponsor branding never overpowers the act or the participant's choice. Sponsor ROI, pricing, and reporting specifics: [NEEDS INPUT].

Available acts represent real, pre-funded delivery capacity agreed with trusted nonprofit partners. ## The Mechanism (for explainers)

Pre-fund -> Activate -> Deliver -> Verify -> Show Impact -> Refill

Funding loop: Fund -> Create Capacity -> People Activate -> Impact Is Delivered -> Capacity Refills

Status stages shown to participants: Activated, Received by partner, In progress, Delivered + Verified.

Reserve, replenishment, and flow-of-funds wording beyond this is **pending attorney review**. Do not publish specifics.

## Objection Responses

| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|--------------------|
| "Am I being asked to donate?" | No. Real acts are already funded and ready. No payment is required to activate an act. |
| "If it's already funded, what does my click do?" | It is funded; your choice gives the go-ahead. A trusted nonprofit partner delivers it, and you see what happened. |
| "How is this different from a donation platform?" | Most giving ends with a receipt. Gratitude ends with a result. Gratitude presents acts, not causes competing for attention. |
| "Can you prove the impact?" | Delivery is confirmed rather than assumed. Status reflects real partner events; only completed stages appear complete. Aggregate numbers: [NEEDS INPUT]. |
| "What did my funding do?" | It created ready-to-activate capacity. Someone chooses an act, a trusted partner delivers it, and Gratitude closes the loop. |
| "Is my funding tax deductible?" / "Is ActivateGratitude.org a 501(c)(3)?" | Do not answer yes. Receipt and legal language is to be confirmed; 501(c)(3) status [NEEDS INPUT]. |
| "Do I have to write a message or take a pledge?" | No. Express comes next and is never required. The Commitment is a shared foundation, not a gate. |
