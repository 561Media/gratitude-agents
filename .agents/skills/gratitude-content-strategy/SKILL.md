# Gratitude Content Strategy

## Purpose
This is the master skill. It holds Gratitude.com's content strategy and can
build a custom content plan for the platform or any campaign.

## Context
Brand memory, positioning, voice, messaging framework, and terminology are
already loaded above. Build every recommendation on them.

## Locked Language (Activate + Fund)
- NOW (MVP): Activate + Fund. NEXT: Express. FUTURE: Facilitate. Never present Express or Facilitate as available now.
- Activate: a person chooses an available pre-funded act. It costs them nothing at the moment. Activate is the dominant action.
- Fund: creates capacity for future acts, once or recurring. Secondary but fully functional.
- Never "donate" or "donation" for either action.
- Sponsors appear only as optional attribution ("Made possible by [Sponsor]") or as one funding type.
- Never invent numbers, results, or partner names. Use supplied facts or [NEEDS INPUT].
- No em dashes.

## For Gratitude.com (Platform Content)

### Audiences
- **Participants (activators):** people who want a simple way to act and to see that their good landed
- **Funders:** individuals, companies, and foundations that fund acts in advance, once or recurring
- **Sponsoring organizations:** create funded capacity for their employees, customers, or communities (a funding type, not the model)
- **Trusted nonprofit partners:** program directors and delivery teams who deliver acts and support verification

### Content Pillars
1. Verified impact stories: real activations and outcomes (demo stories in design work are fictional; say so)
2. The mechanism: Pre-fund, Activate, Deliver, Verify, Show Impact, Refill, and why activation is not a donation
3. Funding that shows results: what funded capacity makes possible, the funder result loop
4. Gratitude as action: the philosophy behind The Commitment (never a pledge or onboarding gate)
5. Transparency: how delivery is confirmed rather than assumed, with privacy and dignity protected

### Platform Strategy
- **LinkedIn:** long-form posts and carousels for funders, sponsoring organizations, and partners. 2 to 3 posts per week.
- **Instagram:** available acts, activation stories, behind the scenes. 3 to 5 per week.
- **X:** short insights, mechanism explainers, gratitude prompts.
- **Email:** segmented by audience (participant, funder, sponsoring organization, partner). Bi-weekly minimum.
- **YouTube / Shorts:** activation stories, platform walkthroughs, verified impact highlights.

### Monthly Content Calendar Template
- Week 1: Impact content (a verified activation story or result)
- Week 2: Educational content (how the mechanism works)
- Week 3: Proof (funder result loop, partner spotlight, verified data)
- Week 4: Action push (Activate first, Fund second)

## For Campaign Content Strategy

### Step 1: Intake
Ask only for what is missing:
1. Campaign goal (first activations, new funders, sponsored capacity, partner recruitment, awareness)
2. Target audience
3. Timeline
4. Current content channels and cadence
5. Budget range for content production

### Step 2: Audit
Review what the user shares. Identify:
- What is performing
- What is missing
- Where opportunities are being left on the table

### Step 3: Deliver
- Platform recommendations (where to focus, where to stop)
- 30-day content calendar (topics and formats per audience)
- Content types by goal
- KPIs to track (activations, funding commitments, refills, partner sign-ups)

## Output Format
For platform strategy: monthly content calendar + platform playbook, as Markdown with a table for the calendar.
For campaigns: 30-day calendar + strategy brief.
